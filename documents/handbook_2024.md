# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (2024 Edition)

## Remote Work Policy

Effective January 2024, Acme Corp has returned to an in-office model. All
employees are required to work from the office five days per week. Remote work is
no longer offered except as a temporary medical accommodation approved by Human
Resources.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. Unused PTO is
forfeited at year end and can no longer be carried over. PTO requests should be
submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 09:00 to 17:00 local time, with attendance expected on
site during those hours.

## Probationary Period

The probationary period for new employees is 180 days. During probation, either
party may end the employment relationship with two weeks of notice.

## Annual Bonus

The discretionary annual bonus program has been discontinued for 2024. In its
place, base salaries were adjusted upward during the annual compensation review.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of an approved submission.
Receipts are required for any expense over 25 dollars.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and two external monitors to every employee. Home
office peripherals such as a keyboard and mouse are reimbursed up to 200 dollars.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited and are grounds for
disciplinary action up to and including termination.
