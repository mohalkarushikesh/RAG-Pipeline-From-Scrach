# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (2023 Edition)

## Remote Work Policy

Acme Corp supports flexible work. Employees may work remotely up to three days
per week, with a minimum of two days in the office. Managers may approve fully
remote arrangements on a case-by-case basis for individual contributors.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. Unused PTO
may be carried over, up to a maximum of 5 days, into the following year. PTO
requests should be submitted at least one week in advance.

## Working Hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours,
employees may set their own schedule as long as deliverables are met and meetings
are attended.

## Probationary Period

New employees serve a 90-day probationary period. During probation, either party
may end the employment relationship with one week of notice.

## Annual Bonus

Acme Corp pays a discretionary annual bonus of up to 10 percent of base salary,
based on individual and company performance.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of an approved submission.
Receipts are required for any expense over 25 dollars.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and one external monitor to every employee. Home
office peripherals such as a keyboard and mouse are reimbursed up to 150 dollars.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited and are grounds for
disciplinary action up to and including termination.
